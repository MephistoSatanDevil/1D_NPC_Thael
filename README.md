# Thael the Thief/Mage and Velvevlos, His Cursed Sword

They can be found near the exit of Irenicus' Dungeon.

**Features:**
- Ability scores: STR 16, DEX 20, CON 16, INT 18, WIS 9, CHA 10.
- He has very little dialogue and no banters, but according to some players, his story is very interesting.
- His "mutant power" is a version of "Tenser's Transformation" that does not prevent him from casting spells.
- Velvevlos is initially a +3 sword that protects Thael from mind-affecting attacks and level drain. It can cast True Seeing 3 times per day and has a 20% chance on hit to drain one level without a saving throw. And it evolves as Thael's story progresses...
- The conclusion of Thael's story takes place in Throne of Bhaal (ToB).
